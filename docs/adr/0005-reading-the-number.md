# 5. A quiet reading note next to the score

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

A score of 6 or 7 is a good result in this method, yet a bare number tends to be read as a ranking. Two seasons with the same score can also be very different, one even across all categories and another with strong highs and weak lows.

## Decision

Under the official score the app shows a short, low-contrast note. Its tone depends on the score range, and 6 and 7 share one tier that describes them as solid. When at least four categories are graded and their grades are very close or very far apart, a second line says so.

The note never changes the score, never appears in the calculation and is kept small so it does not compete with the evaluation.

## Consequences

The score stays a summary and points back to the category table. The wording lives in the dictionaries and can be tuned without touching the engine.
